# Fortune 500 Headquarters Analysis App

A Streamlit web app for visualizing the geographic distribution of Fortune 500 corporate headquarters and uncovering insights into revenue patterns and marketing opportunities.

Link: [Streamlit]([url](https://fortune500analysis.streamlit.app/))
Dataset: [Kaggle](ttps://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mannmann2/fortune-500-corporate-headquarters)

## Overview

### Purpose:
- Explore where Fortune 500 companies are headquartered geographically.
- Analyze revenue trends and identify potential marketing opportunities based on company locations and performance.
- Built With Streamlit

### Features:
- Interactive map showing the locations of Fortune 500 headquarters.
- Data visualizations highlighting revenue patterns across regions and industries.
- Tools for filtering and sorting companies by location, revenue, and other key metrics.
- Insights into potential marketing opportunities based on geographic and financial data.
